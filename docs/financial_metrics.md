# Nita Fleet - Financial Metric Definitions

This document formalizes the definitions and calculation methods for all financial metrics used across the Nita Fleet Management System.

All underlying values are stored in cents (`Int`) in the database to prevent floating-point rounding errors. They are converted to Rand (R) strictly at the display layer using the `formatZAR` utility.

## 1. Gross Income
- **Definition:** Total revenue generated by a vehicle.
- **Calculation:** Sum of all transactions categorized as `Income` or `UberFees` for the specified vehicle and time period.

## 2. Gross Expenses
- **Definition:** Total cost incurred by a vehicle.
- **Calculation:** Sum of all transactions categorized as an expense (e.g., `Repairs`, `Fuel`, `Service`, `Fines`, etc.) for the specified vehicle and time period.

## 3. Repairs Cost
- **Definition:** Subset of expenses strictly related to vehicle maintenance and part replacements.
- **Calculation:** Sum of all transactions categorized under the `REPAIR_CATEGORIES` list (`Repairs`, `BrakePads`, `Tyres`).

## 4. Net Profit/Loss (Net P/L)
- **Definition:** The absolute profit or loss generated by a vehicle after all expenses.
- **Calculation:** `Gross Income - Gross Expenses`

## 5. Margin (%)
- **Definition:** The percentage of revenue that translates into profit.
- **Calculation:** `(Net P/L / Gross Income) * 100`
- **Notes:** If Gross Income is 0, Margin is mathematically undefined and usually displayed as `—` or `0%`.

## 6. EMI Balance (Estimated)
- **Definition:** The remaining principal/interest balance on the vehicle's financing.
- **Calculation:** `(EMI Months Total - EMI Months Paid) * Target Monthly EMI`
- **Notes:** This is a theoretical projection based on the financing terms provided at purchase, not a live integration with the bank.

## 7. Return on Investment (ROI) (%)
- **Definition:** The percentage of the vehicle's initial purchase price that has been recouped via Net Profit.
- **Calculation:** `(Net P/L / Purchase Price) * 100`
- **Notes:** Excludes the ongoing EMI payments from the expense side unless explicitly logged as transactions. Evaluates the asset's overall lifetime yield against its upfront capital cost.

## 8. Revenue / Cost / Profit Per Kilometer
- **Definition:** The financial yield and burn rate per kilometer driven.
- **Calculation:**
  - Revenue / KM = `Gross Income / KM Since Purchase`
  - Cost / KM = `Gross Expenses / KM Since Purchase`
  - Profit / KM = `Net P/L / KM Since Purchase`
- **Notes:** `KM Since Purchase` is derived from `Current Mileage - Mileage at Purchase`.

## 9. Fleet-Wide Totals
- **Definition:** Aggregated metrics for the entire business.
- **Calculation:** Sum of transactions across *all* active and inactive vehicles, plus transactions assigned to `ALLCR` (Fleet-Wide) or `null` (No Vehicle / Miscellaneous). Per-vehicle pages exclude `ALLCR` transactions to ensure isolated tracking, while the Dashboard includes them for accurate accounting.
